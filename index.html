Meet the Candidate

A background in public service and leadership

Senator Shehu Buba Umar is a Nigerian public servant and politician from Bauchi State.

According to the profile provided for this campaign, he was born in Nabordo Village, Toro LGA, attended schools in Plateau and Bauchi States, studied Adult Education at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, and Conflict Peace and Resolution Management at the University of Jos.

  • Former classroom teacher
  • Former Executive Chairman, Toro Local Government
  • Senator representing Bauchi South, 10th National Assembly (as stated in supplied profile)
